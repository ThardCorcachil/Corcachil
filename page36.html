Chapter 3

Page 36

Jonathan had made up his mind.

He chose Mark Ancient as his deputy — the head of the main branch.

It made absolutely no sense.

We had never even met Mark. We didn't know where he lived, or how the hell he was supposed to find out that he'd been appointed.

A few days later, Jonathan announced that Mark had turned down the offer, so we'd have to choose someone else from among our own members.

Meanwhile, I was still looking for Emily's killer.

Not because I wanted revenge, but because whoever had done it could be planning another attack. And they might strike again at any moment.

That made them a serious threat to the entire organization.

Of course, Jonathan and the other Corcachil members didn't seem to take it that way. They announced that the killer would soon be identified and punished.

But the rest of us had given this person a name.

The Poisoner.

Thard and I were working damn hard to find them, especially since the killer was one of our own.

Thard was taking the whole thing very seriously. After all, there was a good chance he could be the next target.

Meanwhile, Jonathan had banned everyone from eating inside the organization. He even shut down the cafeteria permanently.

Well, at least he was taking some action.
