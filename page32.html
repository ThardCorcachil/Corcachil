New Organization Rule

Corcachil is in the process of shutting down its seven remaining branches across the United States.

The organization has decided to establish new branches in other countries:

  • Corcachil Middle East
  • Corcachil Africa
  • Corcachil Germany
  • Corcachil France
  • Corcachil Britain
  • Corcachil Russia
  • Corcachil Asia

It was a pretty ambitious plan. Apparently, the entire project would take nearly ten years to complete.

But here's the thing.

America is the heart of Corcachil.

Why shut down seven branches just to establish seven more overseas?

Personally, I didn't think Jonathan had much of a knack for leadership or politics.

Most of the time, he just sat around thinking or working out. He didn't seem to spend much time making political decisions or actually running the organization.

In reality, Emily and the other branch leaders were the ones keeping Corcachil running.
