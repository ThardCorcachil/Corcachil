But what was the story behind the Ancients?

Apparently, two brothers had played a major role in building up the organization ever since its founding, helping establish and develop its branches.

The older brother was a billionaire and a powerful businessman. Through money laundering and his enormous wealth, he secretly established Corcachil branches beneath ordinary shops and buildings, keeping the whole operation out of sight.

By that point, Corcachil had eight active branches spread across the United States and other parts of the world.

In 1991 and 1992, two of the organization's most important branches were discovered and raided by the police.

All the members of those branches were arrested, including the older brother, Jimmy Ancient, who was apparently the supreme leader of Corcachil.

In 1994, he was killed during a prison altercation.

After that incident, Corcachil decided to relocate its main headquarters to Dallas and operate much more carefully and discreetly, making sure the police wouldn't catch on to what they were doing.
