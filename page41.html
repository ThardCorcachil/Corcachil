Chapter 3

Page 41

Jonathan, the idiot, had decided that Leader would simply be exiled!

Seriously? What kind of punishment was that for a serial killer?

Alright. It was time for me to take matters into my own hands.

Thard tried to stop me a thousand times, but I had to teach that bastard a lesson.

And my idea of a lesson was making sure he never got another chance.

Thard:

"Have you lost your damn mind? Do you think Jonathan's just gonna let you walk away after this?"

Hunter:

"Then get me an escape plan for afterward. I'm getting revenge for all three of my friends!"

Thard:

"You've got some serious guts, kid!"

I didn't pay much attention to Thard's ridiculous warnings. I'd already made up my mind.

Leader was being held in a cell, so I'd need a reason to visit him.

Wait a minute...

A conversation would be a good enough excuse. Jonathan was so damn clueless that he'd probably let me in without asking too many questions.

But there was still the guard to think about.

I had no idea how things would turn out. There was a real chance I wouldn't make it out of there.

But if that was how it ended, at least I'd have done everything I could for my friends.

I had no idea what awaited me afterward. All I knew was that I couldn't turn back.

God help me!
