Oh, God. Their stupid plan was this:

They were gonna spend the night at the Boss’s house so that if Thard showed up, they could arrest him on the spot.

But why the hell did they need me? What was I supposed to do in all this?

Mohammad: Will, I need you—or actually, I’m asking you—to stay away from Michael. I know you two don’t exactly get along, but we just checked his phone records and found out he’s been working with the Franklins. We’ll arrest him after this is over, but tonight, he has to be our bait.

Will: Fine. But what am I supposed to do?

Mohammad: You stand with us and just watch. That’s it.

That’s it?

Did they really think a killer this smart would fall for such a stupid trap?

Up until then, I thought my friend was pretty damn smart.

But then midnight came.

Suddenly, the window of the Boss’s house shattered.

And yep...

It was Thard.

Why the hell did you fall for this trap?

The officers immediately raised their guns at him.

Mohammad: Hands up! Take that mask off! Now!

Thard slowly took off his mask.

I couldn’t believe who it was.

It was Toby...

The same guy who overdosed from taking way too many drugs a few years ago.

How?!

Why?!

Mohammad: Oh my God... I know you.

Toby (Thard): I don’t have time for a long conversation. I’m here for a mission, and I have to finish it.

He pulled out his weapon and fired at the Boss, who was standing to his right.

But the cops immediately opened fire on him.

Michael—the Boss—was dead.

Toby was dead.

Thard was dead.

Was that really the end of it?

Thard...

I thought you were smarter than this.
