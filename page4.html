Page 4

Everywhere on the news, they were talking about a killer named Thard.

“The Hero of Dallas.”

Whatever. I don’t think they saw him as much of a hero.

Everything was going fine until Julie texted me saying she was coming over for dinner. I had no idea what the hell I was gonna do, until I noticed someone had left a strange message in my room.

“Your house will be attacked at 10:45. Get out before 10.”

That was it. Nothing else.

It was our friend, Thard.

He saved my life that night. And since I was scared as hell, I called Julie and said, “How about we go to a restaurant instead?”

She was a little pissed about my suggestion, but eventually she agreed after I promised dinner was on me.

At exactly 10:45 that night, nearly ten members of the Franklins stormed into my house.

They searched everywhere for me, but they couldn’t find me, so eventually they left.

I really wish I’d at least taken my stuff with me, though. They stole a ton of valuable things!

But hell, it was worth it. I was still alive.

That was when I started getting to know my new friend, Thard, a little better.

He felt the same way I did. He knew those corrupt bastards had to be taken out of society.
