It had been a busy day.

0010 had reported a major attack by the Sanichma cartel.

0010: Thard, get over here. This list contains all the information we have on the Sanichma members. I want us to carry out a full cleanup operation by tonight.

Thard: No problem. I'll tell the California branch to get their asses over here ASAP.

So, a brutal war was about to break out tonight.

The destruction of the Sanichmas!

The Sanichmas were one of the oldest drug cartels in Texas. The local police had arrested their members several times, but the cartel had never been completely wiped out. Their operations were limited to Dallas, though.

And honestly, they didn't involve me in the operation.

It wasn't all that exciting, but I had to stand guard tonight in case the Sanichmas tried to attack us.

---

Thard: Alpha team, stay behind me! Bravo team, enter through the back door!

Their headquarters is a commercial building, so don't cause any unnecessary damage. This operation needs to be quiet!

---

While Thard's team was carrying out the operation, I was watching the news. Three hours later, a breaking news report caught my attention.

BREAKING NEWS: Thirty-seven members of Dallas's notorious drug cartel, the Sanichmas, have been arrested.

According to reports, several officers discovered the cartel members while they were in the middle of manufacturing illegal drugs. Nearly the entire group has now been taken into police custody.

Wait... So they didn't actually do anything?

Damn, that's a clever plan!

Corcachil hadn't tried to fight them at all. They'd simply exposed the Sanichmas to the police.

But that left me with another question.

Weren't they worried that the Sanichmas might expose Corcachil in return?
