Chapter 3

Page 29

Apparently, two new members had joined us.

Emily had assigned me to look after them, so I took them to Max to get their identities changed.

Honestly, their situation was a lot harder than mine. I’d been pretty damn lucky that the original Hunter Walker looked about 80 percent like me. But finding someone who looks almost exactly like you? That’s no easy task.

In fact, during the few months I’d been with Corcachil, these two were the only new members who’d joined.

Back in the ’80s, Corcachil had been a pretty damn busy organization, with nearly 3,000 members.

But after the disaster that exposed the organization and led to its complete destruction, the new leaders decided to keep things smaller when they rebuilt it.

Our branch now had 87 members.

The entire organization had around 500 members, give or take.

Honestly, Emily gave me time off every now and then, unlike Thard and 0010, who never did that.

I just hoped Jonathan would let me have at least the same amount of time off.

Sometimes it was three days. Other times, just one.

I usually spent those short breaks traveling.

My homeland, Russia, was a pretty damn nice place.

Of course, I wasn't allowed to contact my old family under any circumstances.

Everything was going great...

Until, out of nowhere, word came in that Jonathan had finally arrived at the main headquarters.

It was six in the morning, and I immediately got ready to go meet him.
