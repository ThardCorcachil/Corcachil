Chapter 3

Page 30

The entire room had fallen silent.

Then the door opened.

Jonathan walked in, wearing an incredibly cold and intimidating expression, with two soldiers following close behind him.

We all bowed our heads in respect.

It felt like a coronation.

He had long hair and looked to be around forty-five to fifty years old.

Despite his cold expression, he was actually kind. He thanked every single one of us.

Then he began his speech.

Jonathan:

"After my cousins, the founders of this organization, were arrested and killed, I fled to the North to avoid being captured.

I want to thank all of you for keeping this organization alive during the two decades I was gone. You've even made it stronger than it was twenty years ago.

Now, I am the rightful heir to this organization.

I promise I'll make this place so advanced that nobody will ever be able to stand against us!

And to celebrate my arrival, I'm giving every one of you a thirty percent raise."

Everyone applauded.

The hall erupted with cheers, screams, and applause.

I clapped along, too, but honestly, that wasn't exactly a great speech.

Why the hell had he come back after twenty years? Wasn't life in some remote village just fine?

I really had no idea what to make of it, but I kept clapping anyway.

Jonathan's style was incredibly old-fashioned.

He was wearing a dark brown suit that looked like something straight out of the 1930s.

It must've belonged to his grandfather. Or maybe his father.

He had a hunter eyes, an elongated face, and no beard.

His expression at that moment was almost comical, but something about him told me there was a lot more going on beneath the surface.

It was as if he'd joined this organization of his own free will.

What about you? Did you run out of money, too?

Still, I was thinking about leaving this organization.

Maybe next year. Maybe in a few months.

That is, if my new boss actually lets me go home alive!
