Chapter 3

Page 45

They took me down to a basement beneath the headquarters.

They uncovered my eyes and escorted me to Jonathan's private office, a place I'd never seen before.

It was an incredibly old and strange room. It almost looked like some kind of nuclear fallout shelter.

Jonathan was sitting on his desk, as usual.

I was nervous.

Suddenly, he looked at me and spoke.

Jonathan:

"Come on, sit down. I've poured you some tea. This time, you're going to drink it."

Hunter:

"Y-yeah, sure. Thank you."

Jonathan paused for a moment. After taking two sips of his tea, he continued.

Jonathan:

"Alright... You've made a hell of a mess. Two people have been killed because of you and our traitorous accomplice."
"Maybe if the previous leaders were still in charge, you'd already be dead by now."
"But I'm not like the others."

Hunter:

"I'm not sure I understand what you mean."

Jonathan:

"Well, here's the good news: you're not going to die. And you're not going to be imprisoned either."
"I've come here to offer you a ceasefire. Does that sound good to you?"

Hunter:

"What kind of ceasefire?"

Jonathan:

"Here's the deal."
"You and Thard disappear. Go somewhere remote, somewhere far away from here."
"And I hope I never hear from either of you again."
"Max will be coming with you, too. We've fired her for helping you."
"You're practically destroying everyone around you. Did you know that?"
"So, that's the deal. Do you understand, or should I repeat myself?"
