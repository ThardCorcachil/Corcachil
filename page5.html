Right now, I’m thinking our new friend’s next target might be Maria, a drug dealer who usually smuggles mushrooms.

Well, technically, she isn’t a corrupt cop or anything. She’s actually protected by the cops, and she supplies a huge chunk of the Franklins’ product.

But taking her out won’t be easy.

Her whole house is packed with armed bodyguards, not to mention all the damn security systems.

Honestly, Thard might need to bring an entire army with him. I don’t know!

But this is where things get interesting.

The next night, while I was cleaning up the mess in my house after the Franklins’ attack, I found another envelope before Julie could see it.

Holy shit, this killer is seriously good. How the hell does he keep getting into my house without me noticing?

There was an address inside the envelope, along with a message:

“Be here at 10:45 PM.”

Well, I don’t know. Maybe the Franklins were trying to assassinate me again, or something?

Anyway, let’s get back to the Boss, who’s pretty damn scared now that the plan to kill me has failed.

Franklin: Hey, old man. This Will Harbor guy is seriously starting to piss me off.

Boss: Maybe your plan was so damn stupid that he saw your men coming and ran away!

Franklin: I don’t know. But you better get him sent to prison, or I’ll turn you in to the cops.

Boss: Idiot! If you do that, you’ll expose yourself too!

Franklin: The cops would never believe your bullshit. You don’t even know where our headquarters is, so how the hell are you gonna expose us? We’re so damn professional that the cops don’t even know the name of our organization!
