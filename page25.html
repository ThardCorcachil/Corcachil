Chapter 2

Page 25

I finally made up my mind.

Look, there were still a lot of things I didn't understand, but apparently, Emily was offering me a shitload of money to help her kill 0010.

Jesus Christ, how much trouble am I supposed to deal with?

Unfortunately, Emily also told me she'd brought the whole thing up with Thard, but he'd strongly opposed the idea.

Well, I couldn't really blame him. 0010 was one of his closest friends.

In the end, I made my decision.

I wasn't going to do it.

My involvement wasn't that important anyway. Plenty of people could get the job done. If I turned Emily down, she'd just find someone else to poison 0010. So I figured it was better to stay out of the whole damn mess.

Count me out.

Honestly, I had no idea what fate had in store for me or the other members of the group.

Eventually, I told Emily I'd changed my mind.

She didn't seem to care much. She just said,

Emily: Okay, no problem. Just sit tight. We'll handle it ourselves.

She went on to tell me that she hoped she'd become the next leader.

Apparently, the most likely candidates were:

- Emily
- Thard
- Mr. Ancient

I still had no idea what was going on with the Ancient family or who the hell Mr. Ancient really was. And honestly, I didn't have a good feeling about any of them.

I just hoped that if one of them took over, at least they wouldn't be another fucking lunatic.

God help us all.
