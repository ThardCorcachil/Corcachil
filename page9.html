Page 9

Exactly two hours later, Thard showed up.

He shot Steven several times, killing him right there. And the crazy part? He managed to get the hell out of there before the cops even arrived.

But how the hell did he get inside in the first place?

The rooftop door hasn’t had a lock for years. Nobody ever bothered fixing it. The building is only three stories tall, so he probably got in using a long ladder or by climbing up the side of the building.

Then he escaped through the rooftop again.

So I guess getting in wasn’t that difficult.

But damn, it was still pretty bold!

There’s one major problem, though, and honestly, it kinda freaks me out.

How the hell did he know the rooftop door wasn’t locked?

And how did he know the layout and hallways of the police station so damn well?

Isn’t that a little suspicious?

I mean, had he been watching the place for weeks?

I highly doubt it.

Either way, he proved I was innocent.

Detective Mohammad should be arriving at the crime scene any minute now, and once he gets here, they’ll finally let me go.
