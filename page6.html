Page 6

Things were getting serious.

I had to listen to the killer, because last night’s attack had been pretty damn serious.

I had an old revolver in my drawer. It belonged to my dad back when he was a cop. When he retired, he gave it to me as a gift.

Figured I’d better take it with me, just in case.

I went outside and got into my car.

Honestly, I forgot to mention this—I’m a huge car guy. My ride is a 1970 Dodge Challenger. I used to have a Charger too, and a Ford Mustang before that.

Anyway, I got in my car and drove to the meeting spot.

You’re not gonna believe this—the meeting spot was Maria’s house!

Oh, and I forgot to mention something about the tape Thard left behind. There was nothing on it except the sounds of the two victims being tortured. But this time, things were different.

Thard suddenly showed up right in front of me.

He was wearing a metal mask and a full metal suit, with two pistols on him.

I just stood there watching.

He knocked on Maria’s door.

One of the bodyguards opened it, and Thard suddenly took out the two guards standing at the entrance with his pistols.

He had suppressors on his guns, so nobody outside would hear what was going on. Pretty damn clever.

His suit was made of bullet-resistant steel and looked insanely heavy. I honestly had no idea how he could even walk around in something that heavy!

Then he got into a tough fight with around six more guards and took them all down while I just watched from the window.

Eventually, he reached Maria.

He pointed his gun at her and said,

“If you wanna stay alive, I need you to start talking.”

Then he pulled the recording device out of his backpack and placed it on the table.

And he got several major confessions out of Maria.
