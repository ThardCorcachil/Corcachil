The next morning, when I woke up, I had finally made my decision.

A car accident.

It seemed like a pretty good way to fake my death.

When I told Thard about my idea, he gave me an address.

Thard: Here. This is Max. She’ll be handling your new identity.

I took the address.

Norman: What about a phone number?

Thard: Just go to the address. We already arranged a meeting for you.

I nodded, still a little confused, and headed out.

It took about twenty minutes to get there.

Of course, I got stuck in traffic too.

No big deal.

Eventually, I arrived.

It was a quiet apartment building.

I found the right unit and knocked on the door.

A woman suddenly opened it with a cheerful look on her face.

Max: Come on in.

At first, I thought she was Max's secretary.

Then I realized...

She was Max.

I was honestly surprised.

For some reason, I had imagined Max as some terrifying old guy.

Instead, she had blonde hair and looked somewhere around thirty to thirty-five.

She also looked like she'd probably been a lawyer at some point.

Apparently, she'd been suspended for corruption and now worked for Corcachil.

Max: Alright. By the end of today, Norman T. Volker is going to be dead, and Hunter Walker is going to be born.

Hunter Walker?

Damn.

That name sounded pretty badass.

But was I really about to become an entirely different person this quickly?

Max sat down and looked through my information.

Max: I've looked into your background.

The real Hunter Walker overdosed by cocaine.

His father wanted to keep the whole thing quiet, so he gave us his son's identity.

And lucky for you, Hunter looks almost exactly like you.

He's the same age as you and about the same height.

She handed me Hunter's ID.

I looked at it carefully.

He was bald.

He had a beard, dark eyes, and was twenty-six years old.

And judging by his records, he was probably around 6'1"—basically the same height as me.

There was just one problem.

I didn't have a beard.

And I definitely wasn't bald.

Max: Come on. Let's go meet the Walker family.

You'll learn more about Hunter Walker that way.
