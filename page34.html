Chapter 3

Page 34

I was on my way there.

I was still thinking about Jonathan's ridiculous new rule. It wasn't even a rule! It was a damn change!

When I arrived, I suddenly found out that Emily was on her way to the hospital!

She had been poisoned. Or rather, someone had poisoned her!

I rushed to the hospital as fast as I could. On the way, I kept wondering who could've done it.

We didn't have any rival groups. Unless someone inside the organization had poisoned her...

When I got there, they wouldn't let me in. I waited anxiously, hoping someone would come out and tell me she was going to be okay.

Could her new boyfriend, Daniel, have done it? He had joined Corcachil too. Maybe they'd had a disagreement.

I saw Daniel at the hospital. He looked even more worried than I was.

No. No way.

It wasn't him.

Then who was it?

Who could've done something like this?

I stayed at the hospital that night, but the next morning, I headed to work.

She was in a coma. They told me she'd be fine...

I hope they were right.

Jonathan was exhausted that night.

He wasn't in the mood to talk much, and went straight to his room.

Then someone came looking for me.

They told me Jonathan wanted to see me.

I hurried over to find out what he wanted.
