She took me to a basement underneath a simple home-appliance store.

The basement was huge.

So huge that I couldn’t believe they had managed to build something like this right in the middle of a major city.

Emily led me to a door.

A guard opened it.

I walked into a place filled with all kinds of strange people.

Everyone was looking at me in a weird way.

Emily: Come this way.

We walked a little farther until we reached a room that looked like some kind of boss’s office.

A man walked up to me, shook my hand, and said:

Man: Welcome to Corcachil, buddy.

Yep.

He was the boss.

He introduced himself as 0010.

He was 5'10", a Black man with very short hair.

They treated me pretty damn well.

Little by little, I started feeling like I was safe there.

Until I suddenly heard Emily and 0010 arguing.
