So... I realized Emily’s real name was actually something else.

Anyway, I found out that this organization—or group, or whatever you wanna call it—had some pretty unclear goals.

It had been founded by the Corcachil family, two brothers named James and Jason Corcachil, back in 1984.

Pretty damn interesting.

This organization had been around for more than twenty years!

I was still listening to Emily and 0010 arguing when suddenly, a man with short hair and a face covered in scars walked up to me.

He introduced himself as Thard, but the members called him Will.

He walked up to me and said:

Thard: Make your decision right now.

You wanna join us or not?

What would’ve happened if I said no?

I thought about it for a moment.

Well... not for very long.

My brain apparently forgot to tell me that I shouldn't say:

“Yes.”

But unfortunately, I said it right then and there.

“Yeah. I’ll join.”

God, why the hell did I say yes?

I had no idea I was about to ruin my life.

I only did it because I was curious.

My life had been pretty damn boring, and I wanted something different.

Thard looked at me and said:

Thard: Come with me. Let’s get you set up.
