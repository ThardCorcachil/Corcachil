Chapter 3

Page 31

Jonathan wasn't much of a people person.

But he did get along with us.

He didn't know me very well since I wasn't exactly high-ranking. In a way, my name had gotten lost among all those members.

Maybe I was the problem. Maybe I just wasn't very sociable.

I don't know.

There weren't any new rules.

Same old routine.

Every morning, I'd get up at six, head to the barbershop, close up at two, and go home.

Then I'd work again from five to seven in the evening.

After ten, I'd spend another hour at the main headquarters, just in case there were any new rules or orders I needed to know about.

Our members didn't use phones.

Hell, they didn't even trust burner phones. The more security, the better.

We operated like we were living in the Middle Ages.

Not that we were sending letters by carrier pigeons or anything! We just didn't use phones or technology for the organization's business.

I did have a phone, of course, but only for ordinary, everyday conversations under my identity as Hunter Walker.

Everything was pretty much normal...

Until, three days later, Jonathan decided to introduce a new rule.

I was really curious to find out what it was.
