The next morning, I went to see Max.

The moment had finally arrived.

She scanned the fingerprint, and suddenly froze in shock.

She turned to me and said:

Max:

"Hunter! This partial fingerprint is a match for Leader Stone!"

And yes!

We'd caught the killer.

It hadn't even been that difficult.

I thanked Max and immediately headed back to the headquarters to deliver the news.

But suddenly, Thard stopped me.

Thard:

"Search the place! Leader has escaped!"

I explained everything to him.

Thard:

"I already knew that bastard was behind it. He was a hardcore supporter of the Ancient family. He was against us. I only gave you those fifteen suspects to throw him off."

Wow!

Thard was incredibly smart.

Anyway, we still had to find Leader.

We searched around for a while, and eventually, our guys caught him just as he was getting on a bus!

Alright, everything seemed to be going perfectly...

Until they took him to Jonathan.

And then I heard something from Jonathan that absolutely pissed me off!

This bastard couldn't even manage to run the organization properly for a single damn minute!
