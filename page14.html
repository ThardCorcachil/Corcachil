I’m originally from Russia.

My grandmother and her family were American, and my dad worked in the U.S., so I grew up there. I know a little Russian, but I’d really love to visit Moscow someday.

Anyway, back to the story.

Emily’s husband saw me every day. He’d give me these weird, creepy looks that honestly made me feel pretty damn scared.

Until one night, when I had to stay there until around 6 PM.

I was heading home when he suddenly stopped me.

Emily’s Husband: Can you do me a favor?

I nervously asked: What kind of favor?

He led me over to his car.

He pulled out several kilos of cocaine and said:

Emily’s Husband: Look at this address on the paper. Drive to this place in your own car. And take this key.

There’s an old car parked by the side of the road.

Use this key to open the trunk, put the stuff inside, close the trunk, then get back in your car and leave.

If the cops see you, just tell them you’re heading to the university because you forgot something there.

It was a hell of a job.

Especially for someone like me, who had never done anything illegal in my entire life except getting a damn ticket.

Should I have said no?

Absolutely not.

Because I was pretty damn sure he’d kill me.

He had just revealed his identity and what he did for a living.

But why the hell did he trust me this much?
