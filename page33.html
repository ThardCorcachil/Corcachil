"Always make people imagine what your next move might look like, not what your next move actually is."

Jonathan Corcachil really admired this quote.

Especially before launching attacks on other places.

All seven branches across the United States had been shut down. The Texas branch was now the only one left in America.

Jonathan didn't have enough time to build the infrastructure for the other branches, so he was trying to buy existing ones instead.

The African branch would probably be established in Egypt, while the Middle Eastern branch would most likely be based in Saudi Arabia.

Still, I didn't think setting up branches in those countries would be easy.

It was getting close to five in the afternoon.

I closed my barbershop a little earlier than usual so I could visit Max. She had paid me $5,000 for a few assignments.

My job was to deliver several letters to some of her people in Alaska.

I got home at around nine that night.

I was about to go to sleep when I got a call. Apparently, it was one of my friends from Corcachil.

Leader:

"Get here. Now! Something terrible has happened!"

I got there as fast as I could.

Leader wouldn't tell me what had happened over the phone. I had to see the scene for myself.
