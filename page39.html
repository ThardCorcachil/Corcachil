Chapter 3

Page 39

Well, the news finally came in.

Unfortunately, we were out of luck. Nova and Daniel hadn't made it. They both died.

But Leader survived.

Thard considered that a small victory. But at what cost?

We'd lost two of our own members.

And let's not forget Emily, our closest friend!

The Poisoner had now committed a third murder. And chances were, Thard and I would be next. As for Leader, well, he was still in the hospital.

Leader was still recovering in the hospital.

I was about to head home when Thard stopped me.

Thard:

"Come with me."

So I went home with him.

He handed me a small piece of adhesive tape with a fingerprint on it.

Thard:

"I collected this on the night Emily died. It's a partial fingerprint. I want you to take it to Max tomorrow and have her examine it and check for a match."

I was slowly starting to understand Thard's behavior.

He suspected us.

At this point, only Leader and I were left.

And he seemed to suspect me more than anyone else.

But I wasn't the killer!

So... could it have been Leader?

I took the fingerprint and told Thard I'd go to Max's at eight the next morning. I didn't think she'd open the door for me again that night.

Besides, I was absolutely exhausted.

The next day, a huge secret was finally going to come out.

But why hadn't Thard shown us the evidence earlier?

I didn't think Leader was the Poisoner.

Why would he poison himself?

Unless...

Unless I'd done it, and somehow I didn't even know?
