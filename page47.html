The Ancient family, working alongside the Corcachil brothers, had established an organization called the Ancient-Corcachil Union.

However, sometime in the mid-1980s, disagreements between Jimmy Ancient and the Corcachil brothers led to the Ancients selling their share to James Corcachil and leaving the organization.

But shortly after the deal was made, Jimmy Ancient was assassinated by Corcachil operatives.

November 21, 2006 — Chicago

Oh my God! Life is great!

Especially when you can get your hands on a whole lot of information about the Ancients with the help of your friend Max, who's all the way up in Alaska!

To be honest, Max can't help me in person, so I'll have to get some assistance from her sister and her sister's husband, who live somewhere near Chicago.

Apparently, Mark Ancient is the only member of the family left...

He lives somewhere around here, and from what I've heard, he's just an ordinary psychologist working downtown.

Here's something interesting I found out about him: he hasn't even changed his identity! He still goes by the name Mark Ancient!

What a gutsy guy! Seriously, what the hell is he thinking?

Well, I should probably thank him. He's certainly making himself a lot easier to find!
