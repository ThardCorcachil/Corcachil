June 15, 2005
Dallas, Texas, USA

Hi.

My name is Norman T. Volker.

Well... that was my name back then. This story takes place around the time I first met Corcachil.

I was just an ordinary college student in Albuquerque, New Mexico. I was originally from Dallas, so it was a pretty damn long trip.

That’s why I usually drove my dad’s car.

My parents kicked me out after I turned eighteen. I was only twenty-four.

They had their own strict rules, I guess. They’d already done the same thing to my brother and my two sisters.

I had plenty of friends, though. Scott and Nolan were my best friends.

Everything changed when a young woman joined our class.

She was about 5'6", had brown hair, and a pretty serious look on her face.

Every time I saw her, I completely lost my damn composure.

But there was one problem.

She was married.

Yeah... she had a husband.

Her husband was a member of a drug cartel called the Sanichmas, a group that was pretty damn well-known across Texas and New Mexico.

The cops didn't have enough evidence to charge or arrest them, though.

Hell, nobody even knew what their real identities were.

Her name was Emily.
