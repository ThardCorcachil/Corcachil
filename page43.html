Chapter 3

Page 43

I finally reached the emergency exit.

I opened the door.

The space inside was incredibly narrow.

I struggled to make my way upward, desperately trying to reach the exit above.

I could hear the guards shouting.

They were getting closer. I had no idea whether I'd make it out in time.

It was terrifying, and every second felt painful. I was running out of time, and the pressure was almost unbearable.

Finally, I managed to get out just as the guards rushed inside.

I immediately started running.

At the same time, Thard was making his own escape.

Unfortunately, he couldn't simply disappear. His situation was just as dangerous as mine.

I really hoped he'd make it out safely.

I was getting exhausted. After a while, I found myself somewhere in the city, far from the headquarters.

I didn't have my wallet or anything else with me.

Going home or returning to my barbershop wasn't an option.

My best option was Max's house.

So I headed straight for her place, hoping she'd let me in.
