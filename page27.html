Mark Ancient, the younger brother, was still somewhere in the United States, apparently, and now he'd thrown his hat into the ring!

But then something happened that left everyone in shock.

A week later, none of the three candidates had won.

After an important meeting between the leaders of all eight branches, they decided to transfer ownership of the organization to its rightful successor.

But who the hell was this successor?

The Corcachil brothers had been dead for years. According to them, ownership had to pass to someone from the Corcachil family, and the best candidate was their cousin, a man named Jonathan Corcachil.

I'd never even known this guy existed!

Emily and Thard weren't exactly thrilled about the decision.

As for Mark, he wasn't even in Dallas.

They announced that, in less than three days, Mr. Corcachil's rightful successor would arrive in Dallas and take his place on the throne!

Apparently, Thard had met him before.

I asked Thard what Jonathan was like.

Thard: His face is so cold and lifeless that nobody dares to talk to him. He's about six and a half feet tall, an absolute giant. The last time I saw him was Christmas 2003. Honestly, he doesn't even look human.

Great. Looks like we've ended up with someone even worse than 0010. Just fucking perfect.

Little by little, I started thinking about leaving the organization. I was beginning to realize that this place wasn't for me.

But how the hell was I supposed to get out?
