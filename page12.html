I looked into Toby’s past.

He...

He used to work for an organization.

An organization known by a strange symbol.

They called themselves “Corcachil.”

Toby was a hitman for the members of that organization.

So he wasn’t doing all of this because of me.

He was doing it for his organization.

They had been following me day and night because I knew about them and was working with Mohammad.

Was I gonna back down?

Hell no.

Actually, Toby wasn’t just killing members of the Franklin gang.

He was taking out his own enemies.

After everything that happened, all the charges against me were dropped, and it turned out the whole thing had been a setup.

The Franklin headquarters was exposed, and they were all arrested.

I went back to work.

I was a city cop again.

What could be better than that?

But none of this changed the way I thought.

That’s when I decided to bring Thard back.

So I met with them...

And joined their organization.

They made me their new hitman.

A hitman known as...

“Thard.”
