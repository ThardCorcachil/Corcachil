Chapter 3

Page 42

I'd made my decision.

I was going through with it, no matter what.

I was nervous, but I knew I couldn't keep ignoring what had happened to my friends.

I headed toward the cell. The guard let me in, although I'd expected much stricter security.

They checked me before allowing me inside.

I was incredibly nervous. I was practically about to pass out, but somehow, everything went as planned.

I approached the cell. The moment the door opened, Leader was about to say something when I suddenly confronted him.

Everything happened so quickly.

But the guard behind me immediately reacted to the commotion.

Oh my God!

Before things could get any worse, I noticed Thard on the second floor.

He had been watching everything from above. He immediately signaled for me to get out.

I couldn't believe he'd come to help me!

And that's exactly what I did.

I had very little time before more guards arrived. I needed to get out of there.

For a moment, I actually felt hopeful about my life again.

I wanted to survive.

And it was all thanks to Thard!

So I moved immediately.

I just hoped I'd make it out safely!
