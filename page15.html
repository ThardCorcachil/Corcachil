Chapter 2

Page 15

I took the drugs and the car keys.

Holy shit, what the hell was I thinking? I’m literally working with a cartel now!

No, no... I’m doing the right thing. This is a matter of life and death!

I got in my car and drove as fast as I could.

It was 7:30 PM, and it was already getting pretty dark.

I reached the destination at around 8.

I dropped off the stuff and headed back as quickly as I could.

Thank God there weren’t any cops on the way.

When I got back to the cartel member, whose name I didn’t even know, he thanked me and gave me a phone number.

Bruce: Call me on this number. You did a good job.

Then he reached into his bag, pulled out ten thousand dollars, and handed it to me.

Bruce: Here. That’s your pay. I’ve got more work for you. Maybe I’ll have another job for you tomorrow.

Oh, and I forgot to introduce myself.

My name’s Bruce.

Honestly, I was pretty happy.

I’d just made ten grand for forty-five minutes of work.

Bruce seemed like a pretty nice guy too. Maybe we could work together a lot more.

And honestly, that sounded pretty damn great!
