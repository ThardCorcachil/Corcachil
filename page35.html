Jonathan:

"Come on, have some tea."

Hunter:

"No, thanks. I'm good."

Jonathan:

"Please, go ahead. It's a gift from an Iranian friend of mine. It smells amazing and tastes even better."

Hunter:

"Alright, I'll have a little. Thanks."

Jonathan:

"Anyway, let's get down to business. Do you know who did it?"

Hunter:

"What? You mean Emily's poisoning?"

Jonathan:

"Yes. I just got a call. Unfortunately, she passed away. I was genuinely upset. She had been one of our most important members.

And I promise you, I won't let her killer get away with this. Not a chance."

And yes! She was gone.

I wasn't going to let her killer get away with it either. I'd find that bastard soon enough, and make sure he paid for what he'd done.

Her funeral was scheduled for three the following afternoon.

I got myself ready and went to the funeral.

Apparently, some unknown family members, who were only her family on paper, were crying beside her grave.

How heartbreaking.

Imagine having a bunch of strangers pretending to be your parents, crying at your grave instead of your actual mother and father.

A week passed after her death.

Corcachil was looking for a new leader for its main branch.

Jonathan wasn't appointing branch leaders anymore. Instead, he had introduced a new position called Deputy Chief, basically his own second-in-command.

It would probably be Thard or Leader. I'm not exactly sure.
