Mark: "Alright, let's start with you. What's your name, and may I ask why you've come to see me?"
Hunter: "Well, alright. My name is Hunter Walker. I've been going through some serious emotional breakdowns lately, so I decided to come see you, Mr. Ancient."
Mark: "What kind of breakdowns?"
Hunter: "I've recently lost a lot of my friends. My family is incredibly distant from me, and I can't see them or even stay in touch with them. My best friend now lives in another country, and I'm completely alone in this huge city."
Mark: "Being alone isn't necessarily a bad thing, Mr. Walker. I'm alone too, just like you. I've never been married, and I don't have any children. I also lost my brother a few years ago in a tragic accident. He was the only family I had left. But I've learned to live with it."
"You know? Sometimes being alone can actually be a good thing. It gives you the chance to accept yourself and understand what's really going on inside your own mind."

Hunter's thoughts:

Well, apparently Mark is being pretty honest with me. He's not lying about what happened to him. That's a little strange.

.

.

I spent almost an hour talking with him before the session ended and I left.

He seemed like a genuinely nice and kind person. But you should never judge someone after just one meeting!

Some people don't show you their true personalities until much later.

My session ended at five in the afternoon.

It was freezing outside, but I waited until he closed his office so I could find out where he lived.

Well, here's the thing: I couldn't find his home address. Even Max's sister couldn't help me with that.

I'm starting to think he actually lives in his office!
