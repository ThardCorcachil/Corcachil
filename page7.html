Thard: Tell me what you really do for a living. And give me your boss’s name.

Maria: They’ll kill me if I tell you!

Thard: I said talk if you wanna stay alive!

Maria: Alright, fine.

I’ve been working for a criminal organization called the Franklins for six years, supplying all kinds of drugs—cocaine, mushrooms, heroin, you name it.

And I know I’m probably not walking out of here after this recording, but I’ve got one message for you assholes:

Fuck every single one of you crazy bastards for forcing me into your stupid little game. I hope every last one of you gets what you deserve.

Suddenly, I heard a gunshot!

I had no idea what was going on. When I looked carefully, I saw Maria trying to escape. Thard fired several shots, killing her, then calmly walked away like nothing had happened.

I counted his shots.

He was out of bullets. I figured he didn’t have any left.

When he walked out of the house and started coming toward me, I got scared and raised my revolver at him.

Will: Can you tell me why the hell you’re doing all this?

I counted your shots. You’re out of ammo, so start talking!

Thard: Heh. I expected a better reaction from you, Mr. Will Jason Harbour!

Will: How the hell do you know my full name, you psycho?! Take that stupid mask off!

But then I decided to let it go, and he slowly walked away.

I came out from behind the bushes in the yard and got into my car.

But I had no idea just how damn unlucky I was.

One of Detective Mohammad’s informants had been following me the whole time. He was watching everything.

I didn’t think much of it. I just got in my car and drove away.

But the next morning, at exactly 7 AM, someone knocked on my door.

That was when they arrested me for the murder of three people.

They had enough evidence to pin everything on me.

Goddamn my luck!
