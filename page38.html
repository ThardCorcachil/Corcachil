Chapter 3

Page 38

It was getting late. I was home and getting ready to go to sleep.

The next day, we were supposed to search all fifteen suspects' houses. It was going to be a tough job.

Then, at around 10:15 PM, Thard called me.

Thard:

"Get here. Now! There's been a second attack!"

I rushed to the scene as fast as I could.

Daniel, Nova, and Leader had been poisoned. They were already on their way to the hospital.

Thard was completely shocked.

How was that even possible?

They'd only had a little water from the water fountain!

Jonathan immediately banned everyone from drinking the water after finding out that the water tank had been poisoned.

I went to the hospital.

The doctors there were our own people, so there was no need to worry about them getting proper treatment.

I waited for almost two hours.

Then Thard came up to me.

Thard:

"The Poisoner knows exactly what they're doing. They're damn good at this. Go see Miss Max right away and ask her to investigate the information I'm about to give you."

He handed me a few sheets of paper, and I went straight to Max's place.

Something about this was a little strange.

The papers had the names of our own members on them.

I woke Max up by knocking on her door. She answered looking half-asleep and pretty annoyed.

Then she started going through the papers.

She dug up all the information on Nova, Leader, Daniel, and me.

She handed me the files and told me to go.

But why?

What the hell did she need my information for?

When I handed the papers to Thard, he glanced through them and simply said:

Thard:

"Thanks."

That was it?
