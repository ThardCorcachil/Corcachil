by Rebin Aghakhani Chapter 1

Thard

My name is Will Harbour.

Date: May 16, 2002 — Dallas, Texas

I'm 34 years old, and this story takes place before I became rich through Corcachil. It happened about a year ago, in one of the neighborhoods of Dallas.

Frankly was a huge criminal gang in the city. They worked with a bunch of corrupt cops to stay under the radar and keep the law off their backs.

I was a police officer myself, and everyone looked up to me. But things changed when I arrested a criminal known by the nickname Dolly, who worked for the Franklys.

My life changed the moment I saw this symbol.

Well... whatever. Let’s get to the real story.

I was just a regular cop until they set me up for arresting a Black street vendor.

Yeah, I know. It wasn’t exactly my call.

But the Franklys saw me as a threat after I arrested Dolly. So they sent some of their top agents after me.

I know what my partner Adrienne did, her piece-of-shit boss Bruce, and that damn smuggler Maria are all about. They all work for the Franklys. I’m sure of it.

Hell, even my own boss was a corrupt sergeant.

And here’s the thing: I was the second one.

My partner Toby overdosed on cocaine last year. I’m convinced they had something to do with it. They just couldn’t kill me, so instead they made sure I’d go to prison, lose my job, and become the most hated cop in Dallas.

Maybe you're wondering if I'm married or single... Well, actually, I got divorced two years ago. My wife didn't wanna deal with being married to a cop who was always getting into trouble.

Turns out, those assholes, including my boss, had covered their tracks pretty damn well.

That young Black street vendor was working for the Franklys. The whole thing was a setup. They wanted it to look like I had caught them, and yeah... they knew exactly what they were doing.

There were barely any security cameras around the place where the vendor was operating. The only one I could find belonged to Mr. Newman, the guy who owned the flower shop. But his cameras automatically delete the footage after three days.

And now? It's been six months since the whole thing went down.

Anyway, I recently met a young woman named Julie. She's a good girl, and she's willing to start a life with me despite everything in my past.

But enough about that.

Everything was pretty normal... until one of my boss's associates, a guy named Adrian, was brutally murdered. Three bullets to the head.

And here's the interesting part: Dolly was upstairs too. Yeah, the same criminal I arrested six mont hs ago— the one they released almost immediately after I got fired.

Apparently, the killer had some serious beef with those guys. People said he beat the hell out of them before killing them.

Then he took every gun they had stored upstairs, along with some cash.

Damn... now that's one hell of a smart thief.

And get this—Maria, Adrian's girlfriend and a drug dealer, was at their place that night for the party. But she didn't get killed.

Why?

Because the killer made his move just five minutes after Maria left.
