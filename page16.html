Chapter 2

Page 16

Things were going pretty well until, two days later, he gave me my second job.

It was pretty much the same as the first one—another delivery of cocaine, although there was a little less this time.

But the address was much farther away.

Bruce: This time, since the drive is longer, you’re getting twenty-five grand. So move fast before it gets any darker!

I grabbed the stuff and drove as fast as I could.

It took a long damn time, but I finally finished the job around 9:40 PM and went back to Bruce.

But he didn’t look happy.

He was on the phone, and I only managed to hear a few words:

Bruce: “Seriously? Right now?... Okay, I’ll try to get there... Wait. Before that, I need to clean this place up.”

What the hell did he mean by “clean this place up”?

Suddenly, he pointed a revolver at me.

Norman: What the hell are you doing?

Bruce: Sorry. I was ordered to clear this place out. And that means I have to clean up.

You were a good assistant, kid.

Norman: Wait! I’ll come with you. You don’t have to kill me!

Bruce: I don’t trust you. You’re just a naive kid.

Suddenly, before he could shoot me, Bruce was shot from behind.

It was like an angel had just come to save me.

I looked over and was completely stunned when I saw who had killed Bruce.

It was Emily!

She had killed her own husband?

Why?

Then she pointed the gun at me and said:

Emily: You’ve caused us enough trouble today. So don’t ask any questions. Get moving and come with me!
