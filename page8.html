They kept me at the station that day.

Meanwhile, Steven—who was actually one of the guys working for the Franklins—was standing there with a damn smirk on his face, staring at me and constantly saying:

“You lost, buddy!”

Well, luckily, there were only two Franklin operatives left: the Boss and Steven.

I just hoped Thard would come after both of them so my innocence could finally be proven!

Every news channel that day was saying the same thing:

BREAKING NEWS:

The infamous Dallas killer, known as Thard, was arrested at his home today, May 25th, 2002.

According to the authorities, he committed his third murder last night!

I was waiting for Mohammad to show up. Maybe he’d actually believe me. I don’t know.

But, unfortunately, he never came.

And that night, that damn kiss-ass Steven was on guard duty.

Couldn’t have gotten any better.

I just had to put up with him for one night in that cramped, shitty cell.

And honestly, I didn’t think even Thard could come save my ass tonight.
