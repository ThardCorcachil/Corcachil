Page 10

Detective Mohammad and the rest of the officers finally arrived and started examining the scene.

I couldn’t see inside the office at the time, but apparently, Thard had left another tape.

This one had a single word written on it:

“CONFESSION.”

When they played it, everyone was completely shocked.

Mohammad: Why do you think he’s killing these people?

Truman: Well, I guess he thought they were bad people, and he didn’t have any evidence to expose them. Right, Mohammad?

Mohammad: So this killer is some kind of hero now? Then what about Steven? Why would he kill a police officer?

Truman: Look, think about it. He’s been killing members of the same department this whole time!

Mohammad: You mean Sergeant Michael, aka “the Boss”?

Truman: Exactly!

Mohammad: You think Michael is his next target?

Truman: It could be a damn good trap.

Mohammad: And this proves Mr. Will is innocent?

Truman: Exactly.

Mohammad: Get him out of there. I need to talk to him.

They finally let me go.

Mohammad: Can you tell me why you were at the murder scene that day, Will?

Well, I had no choice but to tell him the truth. I was just a regular guy. I figured telling the truth was my best shot.

Will: Sir, the killer told me to come to the crime scene. And the night before, when my house was attacked, he warned me beforehand not to go home.

Mohammad: So who was trying to kill you? They weren’t just burglars, were they?

Will: I don’t know their names, but I know they’re part of a criminal gang.

Mohammad: The same Franklins mentioned on the tape... I get it! Michael really is his next target.

Will, if you wanna stay free, you’re coming with me. You’re gonna help me catch this killer.

Otherwise, I’ll arrest you for working with Thard.
