A few days passed before I finally managed to put that whole incident out of my mind.

Then, one morning, Emily showed up at my house and knocked on the door before I’d even gotten ready for work.

I opened the door.

Hunter: Hey. What are you doing here?

Emily: We need to kill 0010. And I need your help.

Hunter: Kill him? Why? What the hell for?

Emily: He's mentally unstable. If we don't do something, we're all going to end up dead.

Hunter: How?

Emily: 0010 plans to abandon our branch and move the main headquarters to Albuquerque. Apparently, he hasn't even chosen a new leader for our branch, and Thard is supposed to take over the California branch. That means the rest of us are about to be out of a job. None of us are being transferred except Thard, and they're planning to kill the rest of us to clean house!

Hunter: Hold on a second. Why the hell are they moving the branch in the first place?

Emily: We relocate every five years, but in the past, they always transferred us to another city, too. This time, though, the main headquarters has been in Dallas for almost twenty years, and that's putting us at a much greater risk of being exposed. Corcachil has plenty of people to spare, so they don't need us anymore. Not even me.

Hunter: Okay, but here's the real problem. How the hell are we supposed to kill him?

Emily: Easy. We'll poison him and make it look like one of the Sanichmas' spies did it to avenge the destruction of their cartel.

Hunter: And who's going to take over after him?

Emily: Probably me, or someone from the Ancient family.

Hunter: The Ancient family? Who the hell are they?

Emily: I'll explain that later. For now, just think about whether you're willing to work with me or not, Norman...

Sorry. Hunter. Or whatever the hell your name is these days.
