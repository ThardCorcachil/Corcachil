Chapter 2

Page 21

After spending hours looking around, meeting Hunter’s family, and all that stuff, Max finally took me to a house in the city center.

Apparently, it had belonged to Hunter.

Well, the old Hunter was gone. Now, I was the new one.

That night, I sat down and shaved my head. Apparently, Hunter had been a barber, and his house was full of barbering equipment.

I was supposed to go to the barbershop the next day, too.

I spent the rest of the night reading through a document about Hunter’s personality, the way he talked, and how he behaved around people.

But I was still nervous.

What if someone got suspicious tomorrow? What if they figured out I wasn’t really Hunter?

The next morning, I headed to the barbershop.

Apparently, my coworker was a middle-aged guy. He took one look at me and said hello, but he didn’t seem suspicious at all. He didn’t even ask why I’d been away from work for so long.

Then, out of nowhere, he walked over to me and said,

Coworker: I'm a member of Corcachil myself. So relax, you're good. Just get back to work.

That was weird as hell.

I guess the late Hunter had been a member of Corcachil, too!

But I still had no idea what this organization was actually trying to accomplish. I needed to find out what their real purpose was.

Then again, I didn't really care all that much.

On top of my regular paycheck from the barbershop, Corcachil was paying me an extra $1,000 a day.

And I was only working five or six hours a day!

That was some damn good money. And from what I could tell, I was going to be making even more in the future.
