Corcachil - Chapter 3

Jonathan Corcachil

Chapter 3

Page 28

A week had passed since the election, and there was still no sign of Jonathan Corcachil.

It was almost like he was a legend.

The Dallas branch was back up and running, and everyone had returned to their old positions. Even me.

Emily had become the new leader of the Dallas branch, and apparently, Thard had resigned. He'd decided he'd rather go back to being a regular hitman.

Jonathan was wanted by the police, so he couldn't fly to Dallas. He'd have to travel by land. And since he was staying in some remote village up north, he still had a long way to go.

Meanwhile, I'd made my own barbershop a little bigger and nicer.

Most of my customers were Corcachil members, including corrupt cops and people like that.

Every now and then, though, some school kids from the school next door would come in for a haircut with their dads. But for the most part, I rarely saw any new faces in my shop.

Still, business was damn good.

My pay from Corcachil had gone up by 20 percent because, after 0010's death, they'd managed to export more cocaine to Mexico and Colombia.

Little by little, I started realizing that the organization's main source of income was selling drugs and other similar goods, working alongside drug cartels.

The Sanichmas had once been one of Corcachil's subsidiaries, too, but they'd eventually decided to operate independently.

They hadn't exposed Corcachil when they were arrested because doing so wouldn't have worked in their favor.

At the moment, Corcachil was working with three different cartels.

But the more the organization expanded its operations, the greater the risk of exposing the whole damn thing became. Just like back in the '80s.

That was why I figured Jonathan might be planning to move the main headquarters somewhere else once he finally made it to Dallas.
