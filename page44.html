Page 44

Max could change my identity and get me across the border.

That was why I headed to her place.

I finally reached her building and went inside.

Apparently, nobody was waiting to ambush me, which was a huge relief. I made my way toward Max's apartment.

I knocked, but nobody answered.

I waited for a little while, then I heard Max's voice.

Max:

"Hold on, Hunter. I'll be right there. The door isn't locked. Just turn the handle, open it, and come inside!"

Wait a minute...

How the hell did Max know I was standing outside her door?

I cautiously opened it. And when it swung open...

Apparently, there was no danger.

So I stepped inside.

Suddenly, one of Corcachil's guards appeared behind me.

Guard:

"Don't move! You're coming with us!"

And just like that, I was caught!

How disappointing.

They covered my head with a cloth and drove me toward headquarters in a private car.

I was absolutely certain that this time, I was in serious trouble.

There's never a happy ending for a hero, is there?

At least I'd completed my mission!

The car suddenly came to a stop.

They took me to headquarters, but why was my head still covered?

I already knew where I was going!
