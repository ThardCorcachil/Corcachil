When I woke up that morning, I got ready and headed to work.

It was almost six in the evening when I finally closed up the shop. I decided to head over to Corcachil to hang out with some of my friends.

But the moment I walked inside, I saw something I wish I'd never seen.

A body was lying on the floor, with a screwdriver sticking out of its neck.

Apparently, the guy was one of the guards. He'd gotten drunk at a nightclub, and 0010 had brutally murdered him for it.

And now, he'd ordered Thard to get rid of the body by dissolving it in acid.

Suddenly, 0010 turned to me.

0010: Hey, you! Get over here!

I hurried over, terrified, and answered without hesitation.

Me: Yes, sir.

0010: Go help Thard lift this body and put it in the acid. It's too damn heavy for one person to handle.

Thard and I carried the body over to dispose of it.

Me: Why did this happen?

Thard: The guy's got serious anger issues. You've only been here a month. You'll get used to the way he acts soon enough. Just make sure you're not the next one.

That night, I couldn't bring myself to eat.

All I could think about was how the biggest mistake of my life had been getting involved with Corcachil.

Why the hell did I come here just for a little extra money?

That man was a fucking lunatic.

The guy lying dead on that floor could've been me.
